You can go from sign-in to a complete first snapshot in a few minutes.

Sign-in, setup, and every page of Retina work on a computer, a tablet, and a phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [useretina.xyz](https://useretina.xyz) and choose email, Google, X, an EVM wallet, or a Solana wallet.

    Wallet sign-in asks you to sign a plain login message. It cannot move assets or approve transactions.

    Retina is in closed beta, so a new account needs an invite. An invite link opens sign-in with the code already applied to every sign-in method. With a code instead of a link, choose **I have an invite code** on the sign-in page. Without an invite, join the waitlist on the sign-in page or at the bottom of [useretina.xyz](https://useretina.xyz), and Retina emails you when signups open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what you track">
    Add at least 1 source. Every section is optional, so use the combination that matches your portfolio:

    - EVM wallets
    - Solana wallets
    - Polymarket wallets
    - Manual positions
    - Physical gold or silver

    You can label wallet addresses, check them before capture, and paste several addresses at once. See [Add and edit what you track](/getting-started/add-what-you-track).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose performance settings">
    The optional **Performance** section controls how Retina explains changes between snapshots.

    Choose whether the headline emphasizes raw portfolio value or investment performance. You can also choose time-weighted or money-weighted return handling, how rewards and gas are classified, and whether Home shows a liquid-versus-locked split.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the snapshot">
    Select **Create Snapshot**. Retina reads the selected sources, normalizes the positions, and checks the result before saving.

    If a provider result looks incomplete or a value changes unexpectedly, Retina shows a review step. You can refresh the affected wallet, exclude an incorrect asset, or keep the result when you know it is correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Your snapshot is saved in the browser and synced to your signed-in account. Home opens with the new snapshot selected.

    The first time a new account opens Home, a short tour points out the main parts of Retina: the controls along the top, Home from top to bottom, and then every page in the sidebar, which the tour opens one after another. Press **Next** to move on, or close the tour at any time to look around. To continue, open **Help** and choose **Show me around**: the tour carries on from the step you closed it on, and starts again from the beginning after you reload the page.

    Sign in on another browser or device to restore the account's saved portfolio. Automatic snapshots stay off until you turn them on: the summary of your first snapshot offers **Turn on automatic snapshots**, and **Settings** has the same switch with the time and timezone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your next snapshot

Use **+ Snapshot** in the app header. Choose the quick option to reuse your saved sources, or edit the sources before capturing.

After the snapshot is saved, open **What Changed** to review deposits, withdrawals, market movement, rewards, and anything Retina could not classify confidently.
