You can go from sign-in to a complete first snapshot in a few minutes.

The full sign-in, setup, and dashboard flow works on desktop, tablet, and phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [useretina.xyz](https://useretina.xyz) and choose email, Google, X, an EVM wallet, or a Solana wallet.

    Wallet sign-in asks you to sign a plain login message. It cannot move assets or approve transactions.

    Retina is in closed beta. A new account may be asked for a beta invite code after sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what you track">
    Add at least 1 source. Every section is optional, so use the combination that matches your portfolio:

    - EVM wallets
    - Solana wallets
    - Polymarket wallets
    - Manual positions
    - Physical gold or silver

    You can label wallet addresses, check them before capture, and paste several addresses at once. See [Add and edit what you track](/getting-started/add-what-you-track).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose performance settings">
    The optional **Performance** section controls how Retina explains changes between snapshots.

    Choose whether the headline emphasizes raw portfolio value or investment performance. You can also choose time-weighted or money-weighted return handling, how rewards and gas are classified, and whether the dashboard shows a liquid-versus-locked split.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the snapshot">
    Select **Create Snapshot**. Retina reads the selected sources, normalizes the positions, and checks the result before saving.

    If a provider result looks incomplete or a value changes unexpectedly, Retina shows a review step. You can refresh the affected wallet, exclude an incorrect asset, or keep the result when you know it is correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Your snapshot is saved in the browser and synced to your signed-in account. The dashboard opens with the new snapshot selected.

    Sign in on another browser or device to restore the account's saved portfolio. Scheduled snapshots remain off until you enable them in **My Account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your next snapshot

Use **+ Snapshot** in the app header. Choose the quick option to reuse your saved sources, or edit the sources before capturing.

After the snapshot is saved, open **What Changed** to review deposits, withdrawals, market movement, rewards, and anything Retina could not classify confidently.
