---
title: "Add and edit what you track"
description: "Add public wallet addresses, Polymarket accounts, manual positions, and physical metals to Retina."
---

Retina needs at least 1 portfolio source before it can create a snapshot. You can combine any of the source types below.

## EVM wallets

Add an address, give it a useful label, and select **Check**. Retina reads public balances and supported DeFi positions across EVM networks.

Use bulk paste when you have several addresses. You can also mark an EVM address for Polymarket tracking.

## Solana wallets

Add and label 1 or more public Solana addresses. Retina combines wallet balances with supported protocol positions and removes duplicate representations where providers overlap.

## Polymarket wallets

Add the address used by your Polymarket account. Retina uses public Polymarket data to show cash, open positions, closed positions, activity, odds, and profit or loss where available.

## Manual positions

Use a manual position for anything public APIs cannot detect, such as:

- A CEX balance
- A private vault
- A niche chain
- A cold holding without supported position data
- Cash or another off-chain asset

Enter a fixed USD value or a token amount that Retina can price. Add the protocol, wallet label, asset class, APY, maturity, and liquidity details when they help your analysis.

## Physical metals

Enter gold or silver by weight. Retina applies current spot pricing when it creates the snapshot.

## Sign-in wallets and tracked wallets

An EVM or Solana wallet used for sign-in proves account ownership through a message signature. It is not automatically the only address you can track. You may track any public address, including a different wallet or a cold wallet.

## Editing later

Select **+ Snapshot**, then choose the option to edit sources. Your saved setup opens before the next capture. Changes affect the new snapshot and do not rewrite older snapshots.

<Note>
Provider coverage changes over time. A successful address check confirms that the address is valid. Review the resulting positions before relying on the total.
</Note>
