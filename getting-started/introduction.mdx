Retina brings crypto wallets, DeFi positions, Polymarket, physical metals, and positions you enter yourself into one portfolio.

You give Retina public addresses. Retina reads them without transaction permissions, normalizes the positions it finds, and saves a point-in-time view called a **snapshot**.

## The basic model

Most pages show the snapshot selected at the top of the sidebar. A snapshot contains the positions, prices, yields, maturities, wallets, metals, and Polymarket state captured at that time.

Create another snapshot later to see:

- How your total value changed
- What entered or left the portfolio
- Which assets and protocols drove the change
- How your allocation moved
- Which positions are approaching maturity

Some surfaces also refresh public data live. The Polymarket page and yield data tell you when you are seeing live information instead of saved snapshot values.

## Your account and tracked addresses

Your sign-in identity and tracked addresses serve different purposes.

- Sign in with email, Google, X, an EVM wallet, or a Solana wallet.
- Add any public address you want Retina to track, including cold wallets and addresses you cannot sign with.
- Wallet sign-in uses an off-chain login message. It cannot move assets or approve transactions.

Retina keeps a working copy in your browser and saves the signed-in portfolio to your account. New snapshots sync to the account so your saved history can be restored when you sign in on another browser or device.

Automatic portfolio updates are optional. You can continue creating snapshots yourself or choose a schedule in **My Account**.

A payment wallet is also separate from tracked addresses. Paying for or cancelling a plan presents an explicit wallet transaction only after you start that action from the plan page. The payment wallet is not added to the portfolio automatically.

## What Retina can track

- EVM wallets and DeFi positions
- Solana wallets and DeFi positions
- Hyperliquid positions found through a tracked address
- Polymarket wallets, balances, positions, and activity
- Physical gold and silver
- CEX balances, cold storage, private vaults, and other manual positions
- Claimable rewards and protocol risk data where available
