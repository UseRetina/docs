---
title: "Beta access"
description: "How to request the password and open the app."
---

Retina is in private beta. The app at [useretina.xyz](https://useretina.xyz) is gated by a password.

## Request access

DM [@UseRetina](https://x.com/UseRetina) on X. Mention that you want beta access. You will receive the password.

<Note>
  Access is granted manually. Response time depends on availability.
</Note>

## Open the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to useretina.xyz">
    Open [useretina.xyz](https://useretina.xyz) in any modern browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the password">
    Paste the password you received. The app loads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to setup">
    The first time you open Retina, you land on a setup flow. From there you add your first wallets. See [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The password unlocks the app on the device and browser you used it on. Clearing browser storage clears your wallets and your access state. You will need the password again.
</Warning>
