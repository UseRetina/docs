Retina separates raw portfolio value changes from investment performance. This matters whenever you deposit, withdraw, bridge, or move assets between your own wallets.

## History

Open **History** and choose **Snapshots** to review the saved timeline.

You can inspect:

- Portfolio value over time
- What your positions earned each day, in the Earnings calendar
- Raw value change and flow-adjusted performance
- Net invested capital
- Best and worst snapshot intervals
- Asset mix over time
- Polymarket and locked-capital changes
- The snapshot ledger, with open, rename, select, export, and delete actions

Use the date range and value or performance controls to focus the comparison.

## Earnings

The **Earnings** card shows what your positions earned, measured from your snapshots, with any borrowing cost subtracted. It follows the date range above it.

With **Days** selected, each day is a square in a month calendar. A darker square earned more; a red square cost more than it earned. Select a day, or hover it on a computer, to see what each protocol earned, any borrowing cost, and how much of your earning positions could be measured. **Weeks** and **Months** show the same earnings as bars, split by protocol. The five protocols that earned the most in the range have their own colour, and the rest share one.

Under the calendar, each protocol's earnings for the range are listed with its [Earned APY](/guides/yield-maturities#earned-apy). Select a protocol to open it. Each protocol's page has the same card for that protocol alone.

How a day is counted:

- Each position earns its value that day at the rate measured over the 30 days before it.
- When a day cannot be measured, the position keeps its last measured rate for up to 30 days, so a gap in measurement does not look like a fall in income.
- A position's first day, before it can be measured, uses its first measured rate.
- A day without a snapshot counts the snapshot before it, for up to a week, and says so.
- A deposit makes the following days larger because more money is earning. It is never counted as earnings.

The card appears once a position has two snapshots taken at least a day apart. In the percentage view, amounts are shown as a share of what earns.

## What Changed

After a new snapshot, select **What Changed** from the app header. Retina compares the new snapshot with the previous one and groups the change into useful explanations.

The review can include:

- Deposits and withdrawals
- Transfers between tracked wallets
- Bridges
- Protocol entries and exits
- Rewards and income
- Market movement
- Wallets or positions that appeared or disappeared
- Differences Retina could not classify confidently

Retina shows uncertainty instead of turning an incomplete explanation into a precise claim.

## Activity

Open **History**, then choose **Activity** to inspect on-chain activity across tracked EVM, Solana, and Hyperliquid addresses.

Filter by wallet, network, direction, classification, or date. Retina removes routine approvals and common noise so the list stays focused on portfolio-relevant events.

## Correct a classification

When an activity item is classified incorrectly, change its category. You can also create a recurring counterparty rule for future matching events.

Classification changes update performance calculations. They do not rewrite the underlying on-chain transaction or an already captured snapshot.

## Performance settings

Open **My Account** to choose:

- Raw value change or performance as the headline
- Time-weighted or money-weighted return handling
- Whether rewards count as performance or deposits
- Whether gas counts as a performance drag
- Whether the dashboard shows total value or liquid and locked value separately
