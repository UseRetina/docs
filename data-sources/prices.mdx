Prices are the foundation of every value shown in the app. They drive the dashboard total, every per-position value, and the P&L numbers.

## Providers

### CoinGecko

Used for spot prices on tokens across EVM and Solana. CoinGecko is queried for the assets Retina has detected in your wallets and on your custom positions.

### GoldApi

Used for spot prices on physical metals. Powers the metals portion of [Asset classes](/concepts/asset-classes).

## Where prices show up

- The dashboard total and the chain and protocol breakdowns.
- The [Yield Table](/pages/yield-table) (current value per position).
- The [Polymarket](/pages/polymarket) view (entry, current price, P&L).
- The [Wallets](/pages/wallets) and [Protocols](/pages/protocols) views.

## Refresh behavior

Prices refresh on the same cadence as position data. There is no separate price feed running in the background.
