The [Rewards](/pages/rewards) page reads from 3 providers:

- **DropsBot**: eligible airdrops and reward checks across wallets and protocols
- **Merkl**: protocol incentive rewards earned through participation
- **Pendle**: unclaimed Pendle-specific rewards on PT, YT, and LP positions

Each reward in the list is annotated with its source so you can tell which provider surfaced it.

<Note>
Retina is read-only. The Rewards page tells you what is claimable and links you to the protocol page where the actual claim happens, in your wallet, on the protocol's site.
</Note>
