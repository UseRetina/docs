---
title: "Perps and staking"
description: "How Retina pulls open perp positions, staked HYPE, validator rewards, and spot balances from the Hyperliquid public API, including live funding-rate APYs."
---

**Hyperliquid** is the data source for perps, staking, and spot positions on Hyperliquid. Retina queries the Hyperliquid public API directly with the wallet address you provided.

Surfaced data:

- **Perps**: open positions with size, entry, mark, P&L, funding
- **Staking**: staked HYPE, validator, accrued rewards
- **Spot**: token balances on Hyperliquid spot

For perp APY, Retina uses the live funding rate from Hyperliquid. See [Position model](/concepts/position-model) for how APY is derived in general.
