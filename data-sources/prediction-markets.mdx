---
title: "Prediction markets"
description: "Polymarket public API for open prediction market positions."
---

**Polymarket** is the only prediction market provider Retina uses today. The [Polymarket](/pages/polymarket) page calls Polymarket's public API directly when you open it, for every wallet you flagged as a Polymarket wallet during [setup](/getting-started/quickstart).

Surfaced data per position:

- Market and resolution date
- Side (Yes / No)
- Shares, average entry, current price
- Value, P&L, P&L%
- Implied Win APR

Polymarket data on the Polymarket page is live, not snapshotted. When you create a snapshot, the Polymarket state at that moment is frozen into the snapshot for the History view.
