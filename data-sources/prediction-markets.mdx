## Provider

### Polymarket

Polymarket is queried directly. For each EVM wallet you have added, Retina fetches:

- Open positions: market, side, shares, entry price.
- Current market prices for those positions.
- Resolution dates and metadata.

P&L and win APR are computed locally from those fields.

## Where it shows up

- [Polymarket page](/pages/polymarket): the primary view, with one row per position.
- [Dashboard](/pages/dashboard): contributes to the prediction markets asset class.
- [History](/pages/history): snapshots include your Polymarket state at the time the snapshot was taken.
