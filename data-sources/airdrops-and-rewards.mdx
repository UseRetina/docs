The [Rewards](/pages/rewards) view is built on three providers.

## Providers

### DropsBot

Airdrop eligibility and claim status. DropsBot is queried per wallet for known and ongoing airdrops.

### Merkl

Protocol rewards distributed through Merkl. Surfaces unclaimed reward amounts per wallet per protocol.

### Pendle

Pendle-native rewards for PT, YT, and LP holders.

## What is shown

- Pending claim amounts per source.
- Estimated USD value at the current price (via [Prices](/data-sources/prices)).
- The wallet that is eligible.

## What Retina does not do

Retina does not claim for you. The app is read-only and never signs transactions. To claim, use your wallet on the protocol's site.
