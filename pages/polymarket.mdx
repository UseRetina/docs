The Polymarket view shows every open Polymarket position across the wallets you have added. Positions come directly from the Polymarket API.

## Columns

- **Market**: the market question.
- **Side**: Yes or No.
- **Shares**: how many shares you hold.
- **Entry**: average entry price.
- **Current price**: the live market price.
- **P&L**: unrealized profit or loss.
- **Days to expiry**: time until the market resolves.
- **Win APR**: annualized return if the position resolves in your favor.

## How positions are grouped

Polymarket positions are tied to the EVM wallet that holds them. The view groups by wallet so you can see exposure per address.

## Snapshots

Polymarket state is included in [History](/pages/history) snapshots. You can open a past snapshot and see the markets you held at that point in time, with the prices and P&L from that moment.
