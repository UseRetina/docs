Risk Scores give you a structured read on how exposed your portfolio is. Scoring is powered by Diapleo.

## What you see

- **Portfolio grade**: an overall score for your portfolio.
- **Sanctions check**: flags wallets or counterparties that show up on sanctions lists.
- **Concentration analysis**: how much of your portfolio sits in a single protocol, chain, or asset.
- **Per-protocol risk**: a breakdown across six dimensions for each protocol you have exposure to.

## Where the data comes from

Risk data is pulled from Diapleo. Retina sends the protocol identifiers it has detected from your wallets and Diapleo returns the scoring.

<Note>
  Risk Scores are informational. They are not investment advice and they do not catch every risk. Use them to find things worth looking into, not as the only filter.
</Note>
