The Wallets view is one row per wallet. It is how you see what each address is contributing to your portfolio.

## Columns

- **Wallet**: the label you set, plus the address.
- **Chain**: the chain or chains the wallet has activity on.
- **Positions**: how many positions Retina found in the wallet.
- **Value**: total USD value held in the wallet.
- **Portfolio weight**: the wallet's share of your total portfolio.

## Adding and removing wallets

You add wallets during onboarding and from this view. Removing a wallet drops it from every other view immediately. Wallet labels and addresses live in browser storage, so they are local to the device.

<Warning>
  Removing a wallet does not affect the wallet itself. Retina never signs anything. The wallet is read-only here.
</Warning>

## Supported chains

Retina supports EVM chains via Zerion and Solana via Helius, Jupiter, Vybe Network, and Moralis. See [Supported chains and protocols](/concepts/supported-chains-and-protocols).
