The Protocols view collapses every position into one row per protocol. It answers: "How much do I have on Pendle? On Hyperliquid? On Upshift?"

## Columns

- **Protocol**: the platform name.
- **Positions**: how many positions you hold there, across all wallets.
- **Value**: total USD value on this protocol.
- **APY**: blended APY across your positions on the protocol.
- **Maturity**: nearest upcoming maturity, if any.
- **Historical performance**: how your exposure to this protocol has changed over time. {/* TODO: confirm whether historical performance is a column, a chart, or a drill-in view in dashboard.html */}

## How protocols are detected

Protocols are inferred from position metadata returned by the upstream providers (Zerion for EVM, Helius and friends for Solana, Pendle for fixed-yield, DeFiLlama for pools). Retina normalizes the names so the same protocol shows up consistently across chains.
