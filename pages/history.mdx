History is the time dimension of your portfolio. Retina stores snapshots locally and lets you scroll back through them.

## What is stored

- **Snapshots**: a snapshot is the full state of your portfolio at a point in time. Total value, every position, every wallet, every protocol.
- **Ledger**: the per-position history that backs the snapshot.
- **Polymarket state**: each snapshot includes your Polymarket positions as they stood at that moment, with the entry, current price at the time, and P&L from that point.

## Where snapshots live

Snapshots live in browser storage on the device you took them on. If you clear site data, snapshots are gone. Multi-device sync is on the [roadmap](/roadmap).

<Warning>
  Snapshots are local. Back up anything you care about before clearing browser storage or switching browsers.
</Warning>
