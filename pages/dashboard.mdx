The dashboard is what you see when you open the app after adding wallets. It is the high-level summary of everything Retina tracks for you.

## What you see

- **Total portfolio value**: the sum of every position across every wallet, every chain, every asset class.
- **Watchlist**: assets and positions you have pinned for quick reference. {/* TODO: confirm watchlist scope (tokens, positions, both) from dashboard.html */}
- **Breakdown by asset class**: how your portfolio splits across DeFi, prediction markets, perps and staking, metals, and custom positions.
- **Locked vs liquid**: how much of your capital is in time-locked positions and how much is immediately movable.
- **Chain distribution**: value per chain.
- **Top protocols**: the protocols holding the largest share of your portfolio.
- **Upcoming maturities**: time-locked positions that mature soon, surfaced from the [Maturity Ladder](/pages/maturity-ladder).

## How it updates

The dashboard recalculates when wallet data refreshes. Prices come from CoinGecko, EVM positions from Zerion, Solana positions from Helius, Jupiter, Vybe Network, and Moralis. See [Data sources](/data-sources/overview) for the full list.

<Note>
  Numbers reflect what the upstream APIs returned on the most recent refresh. If a provider is rate-limited or down, the affected card may show stale or partial data.
</Note>
