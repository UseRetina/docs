The Yield Table is one row per yield-bearing position across all your wallets. It is the view you use to see what is actually earning, how much, and where.

## Columns

- **Protocol**: the platform holding the position (Pendle, Upshift, Hyperliquid, and others).
- **Asset**: the underlying token.
- **Value**: current USD value of the position.
- **APY**: the live yield rate reported by the protocol or by Pendle/DeFiLlama for the underlying pool.
- **Daily income**: APY applied to current value, expressed as a per-day number.
- **Maturity**: the date the position becomes redeemable, if it is time-locked.

## What this view is for

- Comparing yields across protocols on the same screen.
- Spotting positions where APY has decayed below your threshold.
- Sanity-checking that your daily income matches what you expect.

## Data sources

APYs and pool data come from Pendle and DeFiLlama. Position values come from Zerion (EVM) and Helius, Jupiter, Vybe Network, and Moralis (Solana). See [Yield data](/data-sources/yield-data) and [EVM data](/data-sources/evm).
