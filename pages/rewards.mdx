The Rewards view aggregates claimable tokens across every wallet you have added. It is one place to see what you can claim, where, and how much it is worth.

## What appears here

- **Airdrops**: eligibility and claim status pulled from DropsBot.
- **Protocol rewards**: unclaimed rewards from yield protocols, surfaced via Merkl and Pendle.
- **Estimated value**: USD value of each pending reward at the current price.

## How claiming works

Retina does not claim for you. The app is read-only. To claim, go to the protocol's own site or use your usual wallet. Retina shows you what is out there; you act on it elsewhere.
