The Rewards page lists every claimable airdrop and unclaimed protocol reward across your wallets, with a direct link to the protocol page where you can actually claim.

<Note>
Retina is read-only. It never claims on your behalf. The page tells you what is claimable and where; the claim happens on the protocol's site, with your wallet.
</Note>

## Summary cards

- **Claimable Rewards (USD)**
- **Eligible Airdrops (count)**
- **Reward Checks (count)**

## Rewards list

For each reward:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Source | DropsBot, Merkl, or Pendle |
| Wallet | The wallet the reward applies to |
| Claim | Specific token amounts, including any already claimed |
| USD value | Current USD value of the claimable amount |
| Status | Claimable, Eligible, Expiring, Unknown Value, Check Required, or Error |
| Link | Direct link to the protocol page where you claim |
