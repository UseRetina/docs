The Maturity Ladder is for managing time-locked capital. It takes every position that has a maturity date, buckets it by how soon it expires, and shows you the total value per bucket.

## Default buckets

- **Expired**: maturity has already passed and the position is redeemable now.
- **Urgent**: maturing very soon.
- **Near-term**: maturing in the next few weeks.
- **Long-term**: maturing further out.

## What counts as a maturity

Maturity is the date a time-locked position becomes redeemable. Examples:

- Pendle PT tokens: maturity is the redemption date of the principal.
- Upshift and similar fixed-term vaults: maturity is the end of the lock.
- Polymarket: positions resolve at market expiry. Polymarket positions show in the [Polymarket](/pages/polymarket) view; whether they also appear in the ladder depends on configuration. {/* TODO: confirm whether Polymarket positions appear in the Maturity Ladder */}

## Why this view exists

If you run many positions across many protocols, the question "what is unlocking next week" is hard to answer with a wallet viewer. The Maturity Ladder is the answer to that question.
