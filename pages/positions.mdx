The Positions page is the Positions Explorer. One row per position, across every source, every wallet, every chain, every protocol. This is the view that shows the normalized [position model](/concepts/position-model) directly.

## Columns

- **Source**: data provider that surfaced the position (zerion, helius, jupiter, vybe, moralis, ledger, hyperliquid, polymarket, pendle, defilama, merkl, dropsbot, or manual)
- **Snapshot**
- **Date**
- **Wallet**
- **Chain**
- **Protocol**
- **Type** (see [Position types](/concepts/position-types))
- **Token**
- **Value**
- **Asset Class** (see [Asset classes](/concepts/asset-classes))
- **Maturity**

## Filters

Dropdowns above the table, filtering on:

- Wallets
- Chains
- Protocols
- Assets
- Types
- Sources

A search box matches token, protocol, and notes.

## Actions

- **+ Add position**: enter a manual position. Used for anything Retina cannot detect from public chain data (CEX balances, niche chains, private vaults, cold wallets).
- **Export**: download the current filtered view as CSV.

## Dust

A "dust hidden" counter below the table shows how many positions sit below your dust threshold and are excluded from the view.
