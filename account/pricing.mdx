Retina plans are priced by how many addresses you watch and how often you capture them.

<Warning>
Checkout and subscription enforcement are not live yet. These are the published plans for the upcoming paid launch. Beta account limits may differ until billing is connected.
</Warning>

## Focus

**&#36;10 per month** or **&#36;100 per year**.

- 1 address
- 1 snapshot a day
- Every view and supported chain
- Ask Retina, including local mode where supported
- Encrypted cloud history
- Email support

The annual price includes 2 months free compared with monthly billing.

## Range

**&#36;25 per month** or **&#36;250 per year**.

- 5 addresses
- 2 snapshots a day per address
- Everything in Focus
- Priority support
- Direct feature requests

The annual price includes 2 months free compared with monthly billing.

## Horizon

**&#36;99 per month** or **&#36;750 per year**.

- 50 addresses
- 2 snapshots a day per address
- Everything in Range
- Priority support
- A Telegram group with the team

The annual price saves the equivalent of about 4.5 monthly payments.

## Current beta access

Retina remains in closed beta while billing is being completed. Existing beta accounts can continue using the limits shown in **My Account**. A new account may require a beta invite code.

Last verified: 8 August 2026.
