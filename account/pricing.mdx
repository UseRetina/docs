Retina plans are priced by how many addresses you watch and how often you capture them.

<Warning>
Checkout is deployed. It shows only the payment methods Retina has activated, so it can be empty while receiving wallets are being set up. Existing beta accounts already use the plan limits shown in **My Account**, and payment enforcement remains off.
</Warning>

## Focus

**&#36;10 per month** or **&#36;100 per year**.

- 1 address
- 1 automatic snapshot a day
- Every view across EVM and Solana
- Ask Retina, including local mode where supported
- Encrypted cloud history
- Email support

The annual price includes 2 months free compared with monthly billing.

## Range

**&#36;25 per month** or **&#36;250 per year**.

- 5 addresses
- 2 automatic snapshots a day
- Everything in Focus
- Priority support
- Direct feature requests

The annual price includes 2 months free compared with monthly billing.

## Horizon

**&#36;99 per month** or **&#36;750 per year**.

- 50 addresses
- 2 automatic snapshots a day
- Everything in Range
- Top priority support
- A Telegram group with the team

The annual price saves the equivalent of about 4.4 monthly payments.

## Manual captures and recovery

Every plan includes 1 manual snapshot allowance each day. This gives you a recovery capture when an automatic update fails or when you want a deliberate point-in-time record.

Automatic capacity that can no longer run that day can become available for manual use. For example, turning automatic updates off lets a Focus account create up to 2 manual snapshots that day. Range and Horizon can create up to 3 when neither automatic slot will run. Retina keeps successful captures at least 1 hour apart.

## How payment works

Open the stable checkout at [useretina.xyz/subscribe](https://useretina.xyz/subscribe). The page only shows payment methods that Retina has activated.

Supported methods can include:

- Pay from a supported EVM network and asset. The route settles the exact plan price as native USDC on Base.
- Pay native USDC directly on Base.
- Pay native USDC directly on Solana.

Each payment covers the period you chose. An annual payment covers a year and a monthly payment covers a month. Automatic renewal is unavailable, so nothing charges your wallet again on its own. Buying the same plan again before the current period ends adds the new period to the end of it.

Retina verifies the payment onchain before changing account access. A transaction cannot be reused for another order or account.

Once your wallet has sent the payment, closing the page does not lose it. Retina watches the chain for that order and activates the plan when the transaction confirms, usually within a couple of minutes. Reopening the plan page shows the result.

<Warning>
All completed payments are final and non-refundable. A routed payment that fails before Retina receives it may be returned automatically by the routing protocol, and it does not activate a plan.
</Warning>

## Payment wallet and tracked addresses

A payment is attached to the Retina account that created the order. The wallet used to pay does not become a tracked portfolio address unless you add it separately.

Retina never asks for a seed phrase or private key. Tracking and wallet sign-in remain read-only. A payment transaction happens only after you explicitly start it from the plan page and approve it in your wallet.

## Current beta access

Retina remains in closed beta and new accounts need an invite. Existing beta accounts have an assigned Focus or Horizon entitlement, and a new beta account receives Focus unless a different plan is granted. Those entitlements are unaffected when paid plans begin to be enforced. The limits visible in **My Account** are authoritative.
