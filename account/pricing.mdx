Retina plans are priced by how many addresses you watch and how often you capture them.

<Warning>
The checkout foundation is deployed, but public payment methods remain unavailable until Retina's receiving wallets and recurring contracts are activated. Existing beta accounts already use the plan limits shown in **My Account**. Payment enforcement remains off until checkout rehearsals pass.
</Warning>

## Focus

**&#36;10 per month** or **&#36;100 per year**.

- 1 address
- 1 automatic snapshot a day
- Every view across EVM and Solana
- Ask Retina, including local mode where supported
- Encrypted cloud history
- Email support

The annual price includes 2 months free compared with monthly billing.

## Range

**&#36;25 per month** or **&#36;250 per year**.

- 5 addresses
- 2 automatic snapshots a day
- Everything in Focus
- Priority support
- Direct feature requests

The annual price includes 2 months free compared with monthly billing.

## Horizon

**&#36;99 per month** or **&#36;750 per year**.

- 50 addresses
- 2 automatic snapshots a day
- Everything in Range
- Top priority support
- A Telegram group with the team

The annual price saves the equivalent of about 4.4 monthly payments.

## Manual captures and recovery

Every plan includes 1 manual snapshot allowance each day. This gives you a recovery capture when an automatic update fails or when you want a deliberate point-in-time record.

Automatic capacity that can no longer run that day can become available for manual use. For example, turning automatic updates off lets a Focus account create up to 2 manual snapshots that day. Range and Horizon can create up to 3 when neither automatic slot will run. Retina keeps successful captures at least 1 hour apart.

## How payment works

Open the stable checkout at [useretina.xyz/subscribe](https://useretina.xyz/subscribe). The page only shows payment methods that Retina has activated.

For an annual plan or a manual one-month payment, supported methods can include:

- Pay from a supported EVM network and asset. The route settles the exact plan price as native USDC on Base.
- Pay native USDC directly on Base.
- Pay native USDC directly on Solana.

For an automatic monthly plan, pay USDC through one of Retina's configured EVM subscription contracts. Renewal continues until you cancel it. Cancellation requires a wallet transaction that removes the contract's future USDC allowance. Access continues through the period already paid for.

Retina verifies the payment or active subscription onchain before changing account access. A transaction cannot be reused for another order or account.

<Warning>
All completed payments are final and non-refundable. Cancelling stops future renewals only. A routed payment that fails before Retina receives it may be returned automatically by the routing protocol and does not activate a plan.
</Warning>

## Payment wallet and tracked addresses

A payment is attached to the Retina account that created the order. The wallet used to pay does not become a tracked portfolio address unless you add it separately.

Retina never asks for a seed phrase or private key. Tracking and wallet sign-in remain read-only. A payment or cancellation transaction happens only after you explicitly start it from the plan page and approve it in your wallet.

## Current beta access

Retina remains in closed beta while receiving wallets and recurring contracts are being activated. Existing beta accounts have an assigned Focus or Horizon entitlement. A new beta account receives Focus unless a different plan is granted. The limits visible in **My Account** are authoritative.
