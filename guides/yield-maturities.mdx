Retina combines yield and maturity information with each position's value, wallet, and protocol context.

## Review yield

The Dashboard Yield Table shows yield-bearing positions with:

- Protocol and instrument
- Current position value
- **Quoted APY**, the rate on offer now
- **Earned APY**, what the positions actually made
- **Est. / Day**, income per day estimated from the quoted APY

Hover a quoted rate to see the markets or pools behind it and where the rate comes from, such as DefiLlama or Pendle.

Filter by wallet or chain and hide small positions. When a provider does not supply a reliable APY, you can add a manual value.

The table contains protocol positions. Free wallet balances stay in portfolio totals without appearing as yield protocols. A dash means Retina does not have a current reliable rate; stale zero rates, non-positive provider rates, and expired markets are not presented as current yield.

The **Totals** row averages each rate over the money that has one. A position showing a dash is left out, because its rate is unknown; a known rate of 0% still counts. Hover a total to see how much it covers. In the percentage view, the hover shows a share instead of an amount.

<Note>
Estimated daily income assumes the displayed APY remains constant over the calculation period. Actual rates, fees, incentives, and token values can change.
</Note>

## Earned APY

Earned APY is measured from your own snapshots over the last 30 days. For each position, Retina follows how its balance grows, or how much each token becomes worth against the asset underneath it. Deposits and withdrawals do not count as earnings, and neither does the price of the asset itself moving, such as ETH rising.

A position needs at least a week of snapshots before it has an Earned APY. Retina shows a dash when the snapshots disagree too much to support a reliable rate, for example when a token's price jumps from day to day. Altcoins, unclaimed rewards, perpetuals, options, and manual positions are not measured.

A protocol's Earned APY is weighted by position value. It appears only when positions holding at least half of what you supply to that protocol have a measured rate. On a protocol's page, a borrowed position shows what borrowing cost over the same 30 days, listed as **Borrow cost**.

When you view an older snapshot, Earned APY covers the 30 days before that snapshot. Past earnings do not guarantee future return.

## Review maturities

Open **Maturity Ladder** to see every position with a known unlock, redemption, or resolution date.

The page includes:

- Summary buckets by urgency
- An unlock runway chart
- A sortable position table
- Filters for bucket, wallet, protocol, and minimum value
- A dust control

Bucket labels and thresholds are customizable. A separate locked group covers positions that are locked without a fixed date.

## Where dates come from

Retina can obtain a date from protocol data, a market resolution, or a manual position. Check the position details when a date looks unexpected.

## Plan your next action

Use the ladder to find capital that may soon become available. Retina does not redeem, roll, or claim a position for you. Open the relevant protocol separately when you decide to act.
