---
title: "Ask Retina"
description: "Ask portfolio questions with deterministic calculations and optional local or cloud AI interpretation."
---

Ask Retina answers questions about the selected snapshot and supported public market data.

Retina calculates portfolio values with deterministic local tools. An AI mode can interpret flexible wording and choose a validated calculation plan. The model does not invent the portfolio totals.

## What you can ask

Examples include:

- How much ETH exposure do I have?
- Which wallet has the most stablecoins?
- What matures in the next 30 days?
- What changed since the previous snapshot?
- Which protocols are my largest exposures?
- Which positions have the lowest APY?
- What did my farms actually earn, beside what they quote?
- How much did I earn last week, and which day earned the most?
- Which protocol earned the most last month?
- Which of my positions are locked, and how much is liquid today?
- How much have I borrowed, and against what?
- What Polymarket positions are open?
- Which data quality issues should I review?

Follow-up questions can use the current conversation context. Chat history stays in memory and disappears when you reload the page.

## Quoted and earned rates

Ask what a position actually earned and the answer adds [Earned APY](/guides/yield-maturities#earned-apy) beside the quoted rate. A position without enough history to measure shows **Not measured**.

## Earnings in dollars

Ask what you earned and the answer is in dollars, from the same day-by-day count as the [Earnings calendar](/core/history-performance#earnings). Name a window, such as last week or the last 90 days, or name a protocol to see only its share. When a day has no snapshot of its own, it is counted from the snapshot before it, and the answer says how many days that applies to.

## Choose a mode

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Private mode">
    Uses deterministic tools in your browser. No inference service receives portfolio facts. Retina may still fetch public protocol or yield information when your question asks for it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Local AI">
    Improves interpretation and wording with a model that runs on your device. Availability depends on browser support and device capacity. The first use may require a model download.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cloud AI">
    Uses Gemini for harder wording. Retina sends the question and a compact set of relevant portfolio facts. A daily quota applies and the mode is optional.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Limits

Ask Retina answers from the selected snapshot unless the answer explicitly uses live public data. It does not execute transactions, provide guaranteed forecasts, or replace independent financial and security review.

If you name a wallet, protocol, or token that the snapshot does not hold, Ask Retina says so instead of answering about a similar name.

Microphone dictation is available where the browser supports it. Ask Retina is unavailable in the public one-address preview.
