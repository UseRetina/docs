The Polymarket view follows every Polymarket address in your portfolio.

## Live and saved modes

When the latest snapshot is active, Retina reads current public Polymarket data when you open the page, and again every five minutes while the page stays open. Select **Refresh** to read it at once. When an older snapshot is active, the page shows the Polymarket state saved with that snapshot.

The page keeps the same layout in both modes. The top of the page shows when the data was read, or which snapshot it comes from, and a warning appears when a wallet could not be read completely.

## Portfolio view

The summary shows your Polymarket total, the profit or loss on open positions, open value, open cost, cash, and all-time profit or loss, with the profit or loss chart beside it. A bar shows how the total splits between open positions and cash.

Each wallet has its own card under the summary, with its total, the profit or loss on its open positions, its split between open positions and cash, and its cash. Select a card to open that wallet.

Below the cards, the **Positions** and **Activity** tabs list open positions and recent activity across every wallet.

Each position can include the market, side, shares, average entry, current price, value, end date, and profit or loss where public data provides it. Turn on **Combine wallets** to show one row for each market and side held in more than one wallet.

The Activity tab lists the most recent trades and transfers. A count ending in + means older activity exists that the list does not show.

## Wallet details

Open a Polymarket wallet to see its positions, closed positions, and activity separately. Use **Manage wallets** to add, relabel, or remove tracked Polymarket addresses. Select **Done** and Retina reads the wallets again when you changed them.

## Public API limits

Retina reads public wallet and market data. Open orders are not available through the public wallet APIs Retina uses, so they are not included in the portfolio total.

Retina never places, closes, or signs a Polymarket order.
