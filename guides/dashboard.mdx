The Dashboard summarizes the active snapshot. Use the snapshot selector at the top of the sidebar to change the point in time you are viewing.

## Portfolio summary

The top of the page shows total portfolio value and the selected change measure. Depending on your performance settings, this can emphasize raw value change or flow-adjusted performance.

Retina also shows counts for positions, protocols, and tracked on-chain wallets. When the portfolio borrows, a line under the total shows how much is supplied and how much is borrowed.

## What changed

The **Pulse** area highlights important movements from the previous snapshot. Select **What Changed** for the full explanation, including activity, deposits, withdrawals, rewards, and uncertain changes.

## Allocation and liquidity

Use these sections to understand concentration:

- **Asset Allocation** groups positions by economic exposure.
- **Locked vs Liquid** shows how much capital is readily available.
- **Chain Distribution** groups value by network.
- **Top Protocols & Holdings by Value** shows the largest concentrations.

For a portfolio that borrows, these sections show what is held, and the borrowing appears under the portfolio total. The centre of **Asset Allocation** then reads **Held** instead of **Total**.

## Maturities

**Upcoming Maturities** lists positions approaching an unlock, redemption, or resolution date. Open the [Yield and maturities](/guides/yield-maturities) guide for the complete ladder.

## Yield table

The Yield Table lists protocol positions with their value, APY, Earned APY, estimated daily income, and APY source. APY is the quoted rate, named for where it comes from, such as **DefiLlama APY** or **Pendle APY**. Earned APY is what the positions actually earned over the last 30 days; see [how Earned APY is measured](/guides/yield-maturities#earned-apy). Free wallet balances are part of your portfolio total but do not appear as protocols in this table.

You can:

- Filter by chain or wallet
- Hide dust
- Inspect how an APY was matched
- Refresh supported yield data
- Add a manual APY when Retina cannot source one reliably

APY and daily income are estimates. Rates change, and a displayed APY does not guarantee future return. Estimated daily income is earned on what you supply and does not subtract the cost of borrowing.
Retina shows a dash when a current reliable rate is unavailable, including stale zero rates and expired markets.

## Metals and watchlist

The metals summary applies current spot prices to your saved gold and silver weights. It shows only the metals you hold. The watchlist refreshes selected public token prices separately from snapshot values.
