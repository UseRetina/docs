title: "Dashboard"
description: "Read the active snapshot on the Retina dashboard: portfolio total, what changed, allocation, liquidity, protocols, maturities, yield, metals, and watchlist."
---

The Dashboard summarizes the active snapshot. Use the snapshot selector at the top of the sidebar to change the point in time you are viewing.

Two buttons at the top right hold your choices. The gear opens **Settings**, which also links to editing your wallets and accounts. The account button opens **My Account**, with your sign-in, your plan, sign-in methods, signing out and account deletion. On a phone, both are under **More**. Settings lists its sections (Snapshots, Notifications, Portfolio and Appearance) beside the one open; on a phone they are a row of tabs. Retina opens the section you used last on this device.

## Portfolio summary

The top of the page shows total portfolio value and the selected change measure. Depending on your performance settings, this can emphasize raw value change or flow-adjusted performance.

Retina also shows counts for positions, protocols, and tracked on-chain wallets. When the portfolio borrows, a line under the total shows how much is supplied and how much is borrowed.

Beside the total are **Next Snapshot**, **Metals** and the **Watchlist**. On a phone they sit in one row under the total; swipe it sideways to see the Watchlist. The day's snapshot counts are in Settings.

## Percentage view

In **Settings**, under **Appearance**, set **Values** to **Percent** to show every amount as a share of your portfolio. Figures on a wallet page become shares of that wallet, token quantities are hidden, and activity lists leave out amounts and gas. Ask Retina answers the same way. Market prices in the watchlist and the metals summary stay visible. Set **Values** to **Dollars** to switch back. Retina remembers your choice on this device.

**Hide dust** leaves out anything worth less than $1. The switch appears on several pages, including the Yield Table, Positions, Protocols, Wallets, Rewards and the Maturity Ladder, and it is one setting: turning it on or off on one page changes it on all of them. Retina remembers your choice on this device.

## What changed

The **Pulse** area highlights important movements from the previous snapshot. A position you left out of new snapshots is not counted as a movement. Select **What Changed** for the full explanation, including activity, deposits, withdrawals, rewards, and uncertain changes.

**Top gain**, **Top drop** and **Money added** or **Money removed** appear only when the amount is large enough to matter for the portfolio: at least $500, or 1% of the portfolio. On a quiet day the Pulse area shows fewer items.

**Earned** shows what your positions made over the last 30 days, measured from your snapshots, with any borrowing cost subtracted. It is the last 30 days of the [Earnings calendar](/core/history-performance#earnings) on History, so the two always agree. Hover it for the last day and for how much of your earning positions it covers; select it to open the calendar. It is hidden when nothing can be measured yet or the amount is too small to matter for the portfolio. See [how Earned APY is measured](/guides/yield-maturities#earned-apy).

**Next maturity** shows the next date with an unlock large enough to matter for the portfolio: at least $500, or 1% of the portfolio. Smaller unlocks stay in the Maturity Ladder.

**Locked** shows how much of the portfolio is locked until a maturity or unlock date, as a share with a small bar; the rest is liquid. Hover it for both amounts; on a phone the amounts sit beside the share. Select it to open the Maturity Ladder.

## Allocation and liquidity

Use these sections to understand concentration:

- **Asset Allocation** groups positions by economic exposure.
- **Chain Distribution** groups value by network.
- **Top Protocols & Holdings by Value** shows the largest concentrations.

**Asset Allocation**, **Chain Distribution** and **Top Protocols & Holdings by Value** list their rows largest first, each with a bar, its value and its share. A row worth less than $500 and less than 1% of the portfolio, and anything past the eighth row, is added to the last row, **Other**, so the shares still add up; hover that row to see what it holds. History's **Latest Asset Split** is the same list as Asset Allocation. Asset Allocation and Chain Distribution show the same number of rows; the longer list adds its smallest rows to its Other row. Select a row in **Top Protocols & Holdings by Value** to open that protocol.

For a portfolio that borrows, these sections show what is held, and the borrowing appears under the portfolio total.

## Maturities

**Upcoming Maturities** lists positions approaching an unlock, redemption, or resolution date. Its counts match the Maturity Ladder, because both follow the same **Hide dust** setting. Select a group to open it in the Ladder. Under the groups, the next four unlock dates show their amounts and days left. Open the [Yield and maturities](/guides/yield-maturities) guide for the complete ladder.

## Yield table

The Yield Table lists protocol positions with their value, **Quoted APY**, **Earned APY**, and **Est. / Day**. Quoted APY is the rate on offer now. Earned APY is what the positions actually made over the last 30 days; see [how Earned APY is measured](/guides/yield-maturities#earned-apy). Est. / Day uses the Earned APY where Retina measured one and the quoted APY otherwise; the daily figure above the table adds these up. A protocol's page estimates its income the same way. Hover a column heading for a short explanation, and hover a quoted rate to see which markets or pools it combines and where the rate comes from. Quoted rates refresh on their own every 30 minutes, and a rate source that does not answer is asked again within seconds. On a phone, each protocol is one line with its value, Earned APY and estimated daily income; tap a line to see its quoted APY and estimate in place, and select **Open** with the protocol's name to go to its page. On a phone, every list sorts from **Sort by** under **Filters**.

You can:

- Filter by chain or wallet
- Hide dust
- Hover a quoted rate to see how it was matched
- Refresh supported yield data
- Add a manual APY when Retina cannot source one reliably

APY and daily income are estimates. Rates change, and a displayed APY does not guarantee future return. Estimated daily income is earned on what you supply and does not subtract the cost of borrowing.
Retina shows a dash when a current reliable rate is unavailable, including stale zero rates and expired markets.

## Metals and watchlist

The metals summary applies current spot prices to your saved gold and silver weights. When the spot price service cannot be reached, the summary uses the last known spot price. It shows only the metals you hold. Gold and silver tokens in your wallets, such as PAXG, XAUT and tokenized gold funds, count as **Metals** in the asset allocation, History and Ask Retina, next to the gold and silver you saved. The watchlist refreshes selected public token prices separately from snapshot values.

Select the pencil on the watchlist to change it: remove a token with its cross, or type in **Add a token**, which suggests tokens you hold first. The watchlist holds up to 8 tokens.
