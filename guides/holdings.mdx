These 3 views answer different levels of the same question: what do I own, where is it, and how is it structured?

## Wallets

The Wallets view groups value across EVM, Solana, and Hyperliquid sources. Each wallet row shows its label, address, networks, position count, value, and portfolio weight.

Open a wallet to inspect:

- Its value and the change since the last snapshot, above the wallet's value history
- What it earned over the last 30 days, the [Earned APY](/guides/yield-maturities#earned-apy) of its positions (point at it to see the quoted APY), how much of the wallet sits in protocols, what it has borrowed, and its next unlock
- Its positions, each with its [Earned APY](/guides/yield-maturities#earned-apy) (point at it or tap it for the quoted rate; a position with no Earned APY yet shows its quoted rate, marked **Quoted**) and its maturity date. On a phone, each position is one line with its token, protocol, chain, maturity date, value and APY
- **Tokens**: the tokens held in the wallet itself
- **Where it is**: the wallet's value by protocol, chain, and asset
- What it earned day by day, in its own [Earnings calendar](/core/history-performance#earnings)
- **Tokens, past year**: the tokens held in an EVM or Solana wallet over the last 12 months, including the time before Retina started tracking it. A dashed line marks when the wallet was added to Retina. Deposits in protocols are not included.
- Relevant wallet activity

The spot holdings section combines matching tokens across wallets. Open a token to inspect its holders and position details.

## Positions

The Positions view is the most detailed portfolio table. It contains detected and manual positions from the current snapshot, with an option to include earlier snapshots. On a phone, each position is one line with its token, protocol, wallet, value, Earned APY and maturity; tap a line to see the position's details under it.

Filter by wallet, chain, protocol, asset, position type, or source. Search token names, protocols, and notes. You can hide dust and export the filtered result as CSV.

Select a row to inspect available fields such as amount, value, protocol, maturity, APY, liquidity, wallet identity, and source. Lending and borrowing positions also show the health factor, loan to value, and liquidation threshold that the protocol reports.

If a detected position is wrong, for example a deposit you have already withdrawn, select it in the latest snapshot and choose **Leave out of new snapshots**. Retina leaves that one position out from the next snapshot on, in that wallet only, and saved snapshots keep what they recorded. To undo the choice before the next snapshot, choose **Keep it** in the same row. After the next snapshot, the position is listed in **Settings**, under **Portfolio settings**, **Hidden positions**, with **Bring back**. When the source stops reporting the position, Retina removes it from that list by itself.

Use **+ Add position** for a manual holding. Editing a manual position affects the current working portfolio and future snapshots. Saved older snapshots remain unchanged.

When Retina starts tracking a protocol you entered by hand, the Dashboard and Positions view ask whether to remove the manual entry, so the same holding is not counted twice. Choose **Keep both** when they are different holdings. The **Manual** source filter lists every position you entered by hand.

## Protocols

The Protocols view groups the active snapshot by protocol. Each card summarizes value, position count, asset exposure, quoted APY, and estimated daily income where available. A protocol's page leads with its [Earned APY](/guides/yield-maturities#earned-apy); point at it or tap it to see the quoted APY. The page also shows what any borrowing cost, an APY for each position, and an [Earnings calendar](/core/history-performance#earnings) for that protocol. A position's APY is the Earned APY; a position Retina has not measured yet shows its quoted APY, marked **Quoted**, and where a position has both, its quoted APY opens on a point or a tap.

Search by name, filter by asset or chain, and sort by value, APY, positions, or name. A protocol with positions on several chains appears when any of them is selected.

Deposits Retina can place in another protocol's product appear under that protocol. For example, a deposit of Tori's trUSD in an Upshift vault appears under Tori.

A protocol's own token held in a wallet appears under that protocol as well. JLP in a Solana wallet appears under Jupiter Perps with the JLP pool's quoted APY, and wiTRY on MegaETH appears under Brix. When a provider reports one deposit twice, once as a protocol position and once as the vault token in your wallet, Retina counts the deposit once. A Strata tranche token such as jrUSDat appears once, under Strata.

## Export options

The export menu can produce:

- A text file of tracked addresses
- A full Retina backup in JSON
- A filtered positions CSV from the Positions view

Use the full JSON backup when you need a restorable copy. CSV is intended for analysis outside Retina.
