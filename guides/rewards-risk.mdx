Rewards and Risk Scores add external context to the addresses and protocols you track. They do not change your saved positions.

## Rewards

The Rewards view checks supported sources for claimable rewards and airdrop eligibility.

You can review:

- Claimable USD value
- Eligible airdrops
- Wallets that were checked
- Token amounts and claim status
- The provider that returned the result
- A link to the official claim destination when available

Retina never claims a reward for you. Verify the destination and connect your wallet on the protocol's own site when you choose to claim.

Some checks may return eligibility without a reliable USD value. Others may require you to verify the result manually.

## Risk scores

The Risk Scores view uses Diapleo data and has 2 areas.

### Wallet scan

Enter a wallet to inspect available portfolio grade, sanctions, concentration, protocol exposure, and risk-dimension results.

Wallet scanning and detailed sub-scores may require your own Diapleo API key and an eligible Diapleo plan. Generate a key at [diapleo.com/profile](https://diapleo.com/profile), then add it in Retina.

### Protocols

Browse the protocol index to compare available category, chain, TVL, incident, and composite risk information.

<Warning>
External risk data is an input to your own review. A score is not a guarantee of safety, solvency, liquidity, or future performance.
</Warning>
