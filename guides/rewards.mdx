---
title: "Rewards"
description: "Check claimable rewards and airdrop eligibility across supported sources without giving Retina transaction permissions or changing saved positions."
---

The Rewards view checks supported sources for claimable rewards and airdrop eligibility. It does not change your saved positions.

You can review:

- Claimable USD value
- Eligible airdrops
- Wallets that were checked
- Token amounts and claim status
- Where the result came from: **Claim checks** (airdrop eligibility) or **Protocol rewards** (rewards earned by your positions); filter by either
- A link to the official claim destination when available

Before the first scan, the Rewards view describes what a scan finds. Select **Scan rewards** to check your wallets, or **Manage wallets** to choose which wallets are checked. A bar shows how many checks are done while the scan runs.

The totals for claimable rewards, pending rewards and eligible airdrops sit in one row above the list. On a phone, the **Compact** list shows each reward on one line with its wallet, value and status; tap a line to see every column, including the claim link. **Sort by** under **Filters** orders the list.

Retina never claims a reward for you. Verify the destination and connect your wallet on the protocol's own site when you choose to claim.

Some checks may return eligibility without a reliable USD value. Others may require you to verify the result manually.

Last verified: 2 November 2026.
