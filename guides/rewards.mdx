---
title: "Rewards"
description: "Check claimable rewards and airdrop eligibility without giving Retina transaction permissions."
---

The Rewards view checks supported sources for claimable rewards and airdrop eligibility. It does not change your saved positions.

You can review:

- Claimable USD value
- Eligible airdrops
- Wallets that were checked
- Token amounts and claim status
- The provider that returned the result
- A link to the official claim destination when available

Retina never claims a reward for you. Verify the destination and connect your wallet on the protocol's own site when you choose to claim.

Some checks may return eligibility without a reliable USD value. Others may require you to verify the result manually.

Last verified: 14 September 2026.
