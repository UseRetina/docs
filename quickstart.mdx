This walks you through opening the app, adding one EVM wallet, adding one Solana wallet, and getting to a populated dashboard.

## Prerequisites

- The beta password. See [Beta access](/beta-access).
- At least one EVM address or one Solana address you want to track. Public addresses only, no private keys.

## Add your wallets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to [useretina.xyz](https://useretina.xyz) and enter the password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an EVM wallet">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="EVM">
        Paste an Ethereum address (`0x...`). Retina queries Zerion for balances and positions across EVM chains.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Solana">
        Paste a Solana address. Retina queries Helius, Jupiter, Vybe Network, and Moralis for SPL tokens, DeFi positions, and NFTs.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    {/* TODO: confirm exact button label and setup flow copy from setup.html */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Label the wallet (optional)">
    Give the wallet a name so you can recognize it later. The label only lives in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more wallets">
    Repeat for every wallet you want to track. Retina is designed for operators with many wallets across many chains.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Once at least one wallet is added, the dashboard loads. You see total portfolio value, breakdown by asset class, and your active positions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
