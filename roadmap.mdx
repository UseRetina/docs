This is what is planned but not shipped. Anything described here can change. Nothing on this page should be treated as a current feature.

## UI and UX improvements

Ongoing refinement of the dashboard, the [Yield Table](/pages/yield-table), and the per-page views. Density, filtering, and sort defaults are the main targets.

## Deeper Solana DeFi coverage

Direct RPC reverse-engineering for Solana protocols that providers do not surface well. The goal is to close the gap between what your wallets actually hold on Solana and what a generic indexer can see.

## Telegram and email alerts

Notifications for maturing positions. The first cut targets the [Maturity Ladder](/pages/maturity-ladder): get a message when a position is about to mature, has matured, or has gone unclaimed.

## Multi-device sync

Sync across devices using Arcium MPC, so no single server ever sees your raw portfolio data. See [Roadmap toward sync](/privacy/roadmap-toward-sync).

## Public beta

Retina is currently in private beta with password-gated access. Opening the public beta is on the roadmap. See [Beta access](/beta-access) for the current state.
