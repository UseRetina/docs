---
title: "Maturity and locked positions"
description: "Where each maturity date comes from, and how the ladder buckets work."
---

A maturity is the date a time-locked position becomes redeemable. The [Maturity Ladder](/pages/maturity-ladder) sorts every locked position by how close that date is.

## Where maturity comes from

| Protocol / source | Maturity is |
| --- | --- |
| **Pendle** | PT redemption date |
| **Fixed-term vaults** | The lock-end date the vault advertises |
| **Polymarket** | Market resolution date |
| **Manual position** | The date you entered when creating the position |

For positions that are locked without a fixed unlock date (some staking products, some vaults), Retina puts them in the **LOCKED** bucket rather than guessing a date.

## How bucketing works

Every locked position falls into 1 of 7 buckets based on the gap between today and its maturity:

| Bucket | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **EXPIRED** | Maturity passed, redeemable now |
| **TODAY** | Matures today |
| **URGENT** | Very soon |
| **NEAR** | Next few weeks |
| **MID** | Next month or 2 |
| **FAR** | Further out |
| **LOCKED** | No fixed maturity date |

Bucket thresholds are customizable from the Maturity Ladder page.
