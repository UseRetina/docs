An **asset class** is the top-level category of a position. Retina uses asset class as the first axis on the dashboard and the breakdown views.

## The classes

### DeFi positions

Yield-bearing positions on EVM and Solana. Lending markets, fixed-yield vaults, LP positions, staking derivatives. Pulled from Zerion (EVM) and Helius, Jupiter, Vybe Network, and Moralis (Solana), with APY data from Pendle and DeFiLlama.

### Prediction markets

Polymarket positions. Tracked per wallet with entry, current price, P&L, days to expiry, and win APR. See the [Polymarket](/pages/polymarket) view.

### Perps and staking

Hyperliquid perps and staking positions. Pulled from the Hyperliquid API.

Gold and other precious metals. Prices pulled from GoldApi. {/* TODO: confirm exact metals supported and whether they are manual entries or pulled from another source */}

### Custom positions

Anything else you want to track that Retina cannot detect automatically. You enter the position manually with a value, an optional APY, and an optional maturity. Custom positions roll into the same dashboard, yield, and maturity views as everything else.
