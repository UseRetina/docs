Retina's coverage is broad and evolving. This page lists the chains and protocol categories you can rely on today. It is not exhaustive.

## Chains

- **EVM**: Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Polygon, Avalanche, and others reachable via Zerion.
- **Solana**: SPL tokens and Solana DeFi, via Helius, Jupiter, Vybe Network, and Moralis.
- **Bitcoin**: via the providers that surface BTC balances. {/* TODO: confirm BTC coverage and the exact provider from functions/ */}

## Protocol categories

- **EVM DeFi**: lending, vaults, LP positions, and staking derivatives surfaced by Zerion.
- **Fixed yield**: Pendle PT, YT, and LP positions, with maturities and live APY.
- **Solana DeFi**: positions surfaced by Helius, Jupiter, Vybe Network, and Moralis.
- **Perps and staking**: Hyperliquid.
- **Prediction markets**: Polymarket.
- **Yield references**: DeFiLlama, used to fill in APY for pools that providers do not score directly.
- **Risk scoring**: Diapleo.
- **Airdrops and rewards**: DropsBot, Merkl, Pendle.

If a protocol is not listed here but it is something a provider surfaces, Retina will usually pick it up. Coverage expands as Retina improves Solana DeFi reverse-engineering and adds providers.
