A **maturity** is the date a time-locked position becomes redeemable. Retina treats maturity as a first-class field on every position that has one.

## Where maturity comes from

- **Pendle**: PT tokens have an explicit redemption date. Pendle's API returns it directly.
- **Fixed-term vaults**: Upshift and similar vaults publish lock-end dates. {/* TODO: confirm exact provider path for Upshift and similar fixed-term vaults */}
- **Polymarket**: every market has a resolution date. Polymarket positions carry that as their maturity.
- **Custom positions**: you can enter a maturity yourself when you add the position.

If a provider does not surface a maturity, the position has no maturity field and does not show up in the [Maturity Ladder](/pages/maturity-ladder).

## Locked vs liquid

The dashboard splits your portfolio into locked and liquid. A position is **locked** if it has a maturity in the future and you cannot exit it without penalty before then. Everything else is **liquid**.

## Bucketing in the ladder

The [Maturity Ladder](/pages/maturity-ladder) groups positions by how far out they mature. Default buckets are expired, urgent, near-term, and long-term. You can adjust the thresholds.
